\section{Method}
\begin{hintblock}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The method shall provide instructions on how the test is to be conducted, including the segregation, labeling, sampling, and testing of test articles. Ideally this should be done in the most user friendly way and include step-by-step instructions and images of the steps to be performed.
  \item Test methodologies required for the test plan shall be described. Required special measuring / test conditions other than ambient shall be given.
  \item Test data to be collected shall be defined.
  \item Required measuring/test conditions (e.g.\ temperature, relative humidity, \dots)
\end{itemize}
\end{hintblock}

\hint{The acceptance criteria for the test plan shall be consistent with the scope and objectives of the Test Plan. Acceptance criteria shall take into account the measurement uncertainties introduced by the measurement equipment and/or the measurement method.}
